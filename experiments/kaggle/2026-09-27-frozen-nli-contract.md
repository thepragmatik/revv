## Fixed source, split, inference and scoring

Verify the pinned author ContractNLI archive SHA-256 `e03fc77bbf8b53e2976a250e81d8a294bc3d5e5fb014521e477dee9340d6287b`; read bounded **train/dev JSON only**, no test or original PDFs. Of 423 train documents, deterministic SHA-256 document-ID `% 5 == 0` makes **76 calibration documents**; other 347 documents fit the evidence centroids for calibration. The TF-IDF vocabulary uses **all 423 train spans** plus 17 fixed hypotheses, as in the completed lexical evidence control, without dev labels. For the **61 dev documents**, train-evidence centroids use all 423 documents and must reproduce **95.1140%** positive any-gold@5 from the earlier control. Thus dev inference uses the same top-five selected spans. The calibration examples never retrieve evidence using their own marked spans. No corpus text or predictions enter the repo artifact.

Score each chosen span as premise with its hypothesis as second input to the same **frozen** cross encoder. Check logits `0 contradiction, 1 entailment, 2 neutral`; score all 1,292 calibration and 1,037 development document-hypothesis rows × five spans = **11,645 pairs**, max pair length 256 tokens, batch 32; record truncation. Encode every pair symmetrically; no candidate order feature. For each hypothesis aggregate span softmaxes into six fixed values: max entail, max contradict, min neutral, mean entail, mean contradict, mean neutral; append a 17-way hypothesis one-hot. Fit one `C=1`, max-300-iteration multinomial logistic **aggregation head** on the 76 calibration documents only. Evaluate on 61 dev documents only, reporting three-way accuracy, macro F1, class recall, raw-head multiclass Brier/NLL, and document-bootstrap paired accuracy difference to a **per-hypothesis Laplace prior fit on all 423 train documents**, plus direct descriptive comparison with the full-train lexical head's 70.20% accuracy / 65.88% macro F1. These raw probabilities do not constitute independent probability calibration. `NotMentioned` is not equivalent to a neutral score for any one span.

**Decision gate:** advance CER-2 shared-state training only if this frozen cross quality control beats **both** the 68.08% no-reading prior and 70.20% lexical head in accuracy and macro F1, preserves/improves contradiction and not-mentioned recall, and does not rely on severe pair truncation.
